Reviews & Ratings

📚

YA / Thriller

Ace of Spades

Faridah Abike-Iyimide

★★★★★ 9/10

A coming of age thriller dabbling into racism and white privilege with a sprinkle of queer romance all wrapped up in high school drama? Unputdownable and left me wanting more!

Goodness's Rating: 9/10
📚

Literary Fiction

Stay With Me

Ayobami Adebayo

★★★★☆ 8/10

Had me staring at the wall for a while at the end there, moved me to tears a bit too. Devastating and beautifully written, unforgettable characters as well.

Goodness's Rating: 8/10
📚

Contemporary Fiction

The Three of Us

Ore Agbaje-Williams

★★★★☆ 7/10

Complex characters with questionable actions, I can't even describe exactly how I felt reading this book. It was messy, uncomfortable, and very real.

Goodness's Rating: 7/10
📚

Romance

The Charm Offensive

Alison Cochrun

★★★★★ 10/10 ✨

A perfect 10. Warm, funny, and achingly tender. This book made me feel everything. An instant all-time favourite.

Goodness's Rating: 10/10
📚

YA Romance

I Hope This Doesn't Find You

Ann Liang

★★★★★ 9/10

Rivals-to-lovers done so right. Witty, swoony, and emotionally satisfying from start to finish. Ann Liang delivered.

Goodness's Rating: 9/10
