About Me

Hello! I'm Goodness — welcome to my little corner of the internet.

My name is Goodness Ugochukwu — I'm 18 years old, a nursing student, and a lover of books in every form. The Worn Page is where I share my honest thoughts on everything I read.

I read across every genre — contemporary fiction, literary fiction, romance, thrillers, YA, Nigerian literature — though I'll admit fantasy and I have a slightly complicated relationship. I'll still read it, I'll just side-eye it a little first.

Outside of reading, I write occasionally, paint, and generally gravitate towards anything creative and artsy. Nursing keeps me busy but books keep me sane.

My favourite quote — which you've probably seen on the homepage — is from The Perks of Being a Wallflower by Stephen Chbosky: "We accept the love we think we deserve." It's lived in my head since I first read it.

Want to connect? Find me on Instagram @goodyoriginal_ or send me a message on the contact page. Happy reading! 📚
